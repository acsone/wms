================
Sale Consignment
================

Manage stock consignment at customer location.

Mark your Sales Order as "for consignment" and the destination will be the
customer consignment location.
If the customer does not have a consignment location, a new location is created
and attached to the customer as consignment location.

As the destination location is not a customer location, the delivered quantity
will not increase. And so, there won't be any invoiceable quantity.
