===============================
ALC RECEPTION PHARMACY PRINTING
===============================

Allows to print the labels of a reception pharmacy.

CONFIG
------

* Enable the "Printing / Print User" option under access rights to give users
  the ability to view the print menu.
* Define one or more print servers and their printers
   (*settings - Printing - Servers*).
* Customize reports as wanted (*settings - Printing - Reports*) through their
  *Print* tab where you can also define specific actions per user.

USAGE
-----

Just click the print button on a reception pharmacy line.
