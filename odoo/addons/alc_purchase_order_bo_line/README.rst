==========================
Alc Purchase Order Bo Line
==========================

With this module, you can now easily identify purchase lines for products that
do not have a usable quantity available at the moment.
This is achieved through the use of flags on purchase orders and purchase lines.

Ho to test?
-----------

- A new filter is added to the purchase search view called "In BO"
- A BO line is colored different in purchase form view
