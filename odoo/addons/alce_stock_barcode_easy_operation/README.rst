=================================
Alce Stock Barcode Easy Operation
=================================

Ease picking operation processing by the barcode app.
 * Skips the JS dedicated interface
 * Processes lot without requiring to open the pack lot wizard
 * Auto increment product and package,
 * Sets quantities all at once in a picking
 * Gives warnings if bad product, lot, too many quantities scanned
